# hijab-for-eye v2.3.2 - Consistent exponential sliders

All sliders now increase slowly at first and gradually faster toward the right. This applies to image and video blur, image thresholds, eye and re-blur icon sizes, all opacity controls, durations, and temporary per-video blur.

Saved values and maximum limits stay unchanged. Slider handles may move to reflect the new curve. The image reveal fix from v2.3.1 is retained.

## Update

Download hijab-for-eye-v2.3.2.zip, extract it, and replace the files in your existing extension folder. Open chrome://extensions or edge://extensions, click Reload, confirm version 2.3.2, then refresh website tabs. See README.md for beginner installation instructions.

## Validation

Automated settings, popup, lifecycle, image, and video suites cover curve acceleration, endpoints, round trips, and existing behavior. Live browser verification remains outstanding.
