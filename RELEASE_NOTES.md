# hijab-for-eye v2.3.3 - Video eye on hover

The video blur-adjustment eye now appears only while your mouse is inside that video's area. It hides when you leave the video or switch away from the browser window. Each video tracks its own area.

An already-open slider panel keeps its position and existing hide-delay behavior. Temporary blur values and the slow-to-fast exponential sliders are unchanged.

## Update

Download hijab-for-eye-v2.3.3.zip, extract it, and replace the files in your existing extension folder. Open chrome://extensions or edge://extensions, click Reload, confirm version 2.3.3, then refresh website tabs.

## Validation

Automated settings, popup, lifecycle, image, and video suites pass. Video tests cover the initially hidden eye, movement between separate videos, leaving the page, and panel dismissal. Live browser visual verification remains outstanding.
