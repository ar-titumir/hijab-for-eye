hijab-for-eye

Soften videos and images across the web.

Where to blur

Turn off All sites before choosing Only these sites. All sites can be combined with exclusions. Domains include their subdomains; overlapping entries are removed from the other list. Rules apply to the top-level website, including embedded players.

On-video controls

Click the eye at the top of a video for a temporary blur slider. It closes after the selected delay outside the video or controls, or immediately when you click outside the controls. Keyboard focus keeps it open.

Filter images by

After revealing an image, briefly show a crossed-eye button to blur it again. The image stays clear when the timer expires. 0 ms means no re-blur button.

Loading settings…

Only images greater than the displayed-size threshold blur. Size, blur and time sliders start quickly, then slow down. Opacity starts slowly, then accelerates.
